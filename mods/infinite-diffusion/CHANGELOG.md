# Changelog — pwc.infinite-diffusion

## 1.1.0 — the cube-planet universe

- Selects the v4 generator (worldgen 7): a 50,000,000-block cube planet with themed faces, round
  worlds on curved charts, moons and asteroid swarms; gravity comes from the matter.
- Four new knobs: Variety, Features, Structures, Deep (all 0–200 %, default 100 %). Old payloads
  load with the new knobs at their defaults.
