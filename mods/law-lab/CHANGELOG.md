# Changelog — pwc.law-lab

## 0.1.0

- First release. `/law` lists the candidate fundamental laws; `/law <id> [side]` runs one on a
  copy of the world around you; `/poke` (or `P`) acts on the looked-at cell; `/law rewind`,
  `pause`, `speed`, `depth`, `ledger` and `off`. A HUD heat map shows the slice you stand on.
- Never edits the world. Requires `pwc-api ^3.1`, `pwc.commands ^1.0` and the six `pwc.law-*`
  candidate libraries.
